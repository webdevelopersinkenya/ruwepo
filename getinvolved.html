Donate

We are committed to responsible management of all donated funds. Like the child on the seashore trying to save as many starfish as possible, we may not be able to 'save' everyone, but we can help bring about needed change for families & thus communities striving to improve their lives. We commit to maximize our effectiveness and invite you to help us by getting involved.

Volunteer

RUWEPO is always looking for volunteers. Opportunities are available for interns to help with fundraising, marketing, and social media efforts. As needed, we may have opportunities to help send items overseas to needing families and children. If you're interested in volunteering, click our 'Volunteer' button and fill out the contact form.

PARTNERTRIPS

We have changed the name of our 'service expeditions' to PartnerTrips.

As needed, RUWEPO develops and organizes teams of volunteers to partner with indigenous groups & organizations in Africa. Each unique team focuses on the needs of the people we serve. They assist RTC's mission through international hands-on service; by implementing its humanitarian projects and programs. If serving appeals to you, click here.
