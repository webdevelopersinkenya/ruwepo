HELPING PEOPLE TO HELP THEMSELVES

RUWEPO works in the slums of Kenya — reaching orphans, widows, people living with disabilities, and vulnerable families who have been left behind. Our mission is simple: empower people to become self-reliant by giving them the tools, skills, and support they need to lift themselves out of poverty.

We run community-based projects that focus on education, health, life-skills, micro-enterprise, water and agriculture, and orphan care. Every program is designed with the community, not just for it — because lasting change only happens when people are part of the solution.

Our work starts with a single person. A widow who learns to grow vegetables. A child who returns to school. A young mother who starts a small business. A person with a disability who finds dignity and purpose. These individual stories, multiplied across families and neighborhoods, are how entire communities begin to rise.

RUWEPO believes in practical, sustainable solutions. We don't just hand out help — we walk alongside people as they build a better future. Please help us continue this work.

Clean Water for Every Family

In Kenya's rural and informal settlements, thousands of families still rely on dirty, contaminated water sources. Many walk long distances every day just to collect water that isn't safe to drink.

RUWEPO works with local communities to protect springs, harvest rainwater, and provide clean, safe water for drinking, cooking, and personal hygiene. Clean water also supports small kitchen gardens and farm animals — helping families grow their own food and build sustainable livelihoods.

Every clean water project we complete serves hundreds of people. Together, we can bring safe water to more families across Kenya.

Ongoing Projects

EDUCATION

Many children in Kenya's slums drop out of school because their families cannot afford fees, uniforms, or supplies. RUWEPO supports orphans and vulnerable children with school fees, learning materials, and mentorship — helping them stay in school and build a brighter future.

Donate

WATER AND AGRICULTURE

Access to clean water changes everything. RUWEPO protects springs, installs water harvesting systems, and trains families in small-scale farming. With clean water and farming skills, families can grow food, raise animals, and earn a living.

Donate

HEALTH

When a child is sick, they cannot attend school. When a mother is unwell, the whole family suffers. RUWEPO supports health outreach, vaccinations, medical check-ups, and health education for families in the slums — because good health is the foundation of a good life.
