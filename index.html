PROGRAMS

RUWEPO operates exclusively in Kenya, focusing on the slums where we empower communities, orphans, widows, and people living with disabilities. Our programs are built on principles of self-reliance with a "teach them to fish" approach, helping individuals and families determine what they truly need to succeed. We use a holistic approach across Life-Skills training, Education, Health, Orphan Care, Micro-Enterprise, Water & Agriculture, and Community Empowerment.

POWERFUL PROJECTS

We facilitate holistic projects that save lives, teach skills, and create opportunities for lifelong self-reliance among the vulnerable communities we serve in Kenya. Our projects range from short-term to long-term, donor-specific to general ongoing campaigns — giving everyone who wants to make a difference a chance to help. We invite you to select a Powerful Project that appeals to you and DONATE to get it done!

GET INVOLVED

We always strive to be responsible in our management of all donated funds. We cannot solve all the problems or help everyone — we are like the child on the seashore trying to save as many starfish as we can. But we commit to maximize our effectiveness and invite you to join us in empowering orphans, widows, people with disabilities, and struggling families in Kenya's slums. Together, we can save many more starfish.
