RUWEPO has standard processes to implement our various programs. We base these processes on principles of self-reliance, sustainability, respect for the dignity of the individual, comprehensive community development, affirmative action for women and girls, and support of the traditional nuclear family. Our work focuses on empowering communities, orphans, widows, and people living with disabilities in Kenya's slums.
